## 2024-07-30 - Consistent Accessibility Properties on Interactive Elements\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons or generic-text buttons (like "Remove" or "Edit") lack context for screen readers when removed from their surrounding visual hierarchy. While a button may visually imply "Edit Household Name," the screen reader just says "Edit button."\n**Action:** Always provide explicit, context-rich `accessibilityLabel` properties (e.g., "Edit household name", "Remove member") and ensure `accessibilityRole="button"` is set on custom `Pressable` or `Button` wrappers so they are correctly identified by assistive technologies.
