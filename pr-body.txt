💡 What: Replaced 14 separate `Array.prototype.reduce()` calls spanning `utils/nutritionAggregation.ts` and `app/profile/nutrition-report.tsx` with consolidated, single-pass `for` loops.
🎯 Why: Nutrition calculation is computationally intensive, particularly when analyzing history spanning hundreds of aggregated ingredients or weekly logs. The original implementation called `.reduce()` up to five times across identical data structures, allocating intermediate closures and enforcing O(5N) time complexity where O(N) was possible. This reduces garbage collection overhead and significantly improves computation speed.
📊 Impact: Reduces time complexity from O(k*N) to O(N) and drastically reduces closure allocations on the hot path for nutrition computations.
🔬 Measurement: Verify changes pass `bun run test` and observe performance metrics in test outputs for aggregations.
